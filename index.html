How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Glowstep. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I bought Premium – how do I get it back?

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap «Restore purchases». Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Go to Profile → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app.

Does Glowstep rate my looks?

No. Glowstep never gives scores or rankings. It gives you a plan with concrete steps, and coaches you day by day.

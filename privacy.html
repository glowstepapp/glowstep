Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Glowstep is built so you stay in control of your own data. In short: everything you put into the app stays on your phone – except when you choose to use the AI scan or «Adjust your plan».

What the app stores

Your quiz answers, your first name if you enter it, your progress (tasks, streak, level, badges, habits), progress photos you take in the app, and settings like reminders. If you choose a photo from your photo library for the AI scan instead, it’s used only for that scan – the app doesn’t keep a copy.

Where it’s stored

Everything is stored only on your phone. You don’t need an account, and we have no database with your data. Progress photos are kept in the app’s own folder and don’t show up in your camera roll.

AI scan, «Adjust your plan» and the weekly check-in

Only when you tap the button yourself: the photo (made smaller on your phone) or the text you write is sent encrypted to our server at Supabase in the EU, and on to the AI service Claude by Anthropic, together with your quiz answers and your plan. It’s only used to create the answer and is not stored by us. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days and doesn’t use it to train AI. The result is stored only on your phone. To limit use, the app sends a random ID for the phone (not personal), and we count how many AI answers it has received per day.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to create them.

Sharing

When you tap «Share», an image of the share card is made on your phone, and your phone’s own share menu opens. You decide what is shared and where.

Purchases

The subscription is bought and paid for through Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Tracking and ads

Glowstep uses no tracking, no analytics tools and no ad networks.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time under Profile → Delete all data. If you delete the app, everything is gone too.

Age limit

Glowstep is for people aged 18 and over.
